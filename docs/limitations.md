
# Limitations and Responsible Use

- This is an educational machine-learning prototype.
- It is not approved or validated for clinical use.
- Predictions must not be used to diagnose or treat patients.
- Training data may contain bias, labeling errors, or artifacts.
- Test-set performance may not generalize to other hospitals,
  populations, scanners, or imaging protocols.
- Model scores are not necessarily calibrated probabilities.
- No claim of clinical safety, diagnostic accuracy, or
  suitability for patient care is made.
- Use only public datasets according to their terms.
- Do not upload identifiable patient images or protected health
  information to the demonstration application.
- Clinical use would require independent validation, appropriate
  governance, security controls, regulatory assessment, and
  qualified clinical oversight.
